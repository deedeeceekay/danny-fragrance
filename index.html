FAQ

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch from our UK warehouse within 1 to 2 working days. Standard UK delivery then takes 2 to 4 working days.

Can I return my order?

Yes. Unused, unopened items can be returned within 30 days.

Are your fragrances genuine?

Yes. Everything we sell is the original product from the brand, in its sealed retail packaging.

How long do the scents last?

Most of our eau de parfums last 6 to 8 hours on skin, and extraits usually longer. Spraying onto moisturised skin helps it last.

Do you sell samples?

Not yet. If you’re unsure, tell us the notes you like and we’ll suggest a match.
